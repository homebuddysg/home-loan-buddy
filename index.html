Why Home Loan Buddy exists

Home loans can be difficult to understand, especially when product pages use technical language or focus only on headline rates.

Home Loan Buddy is an independent educational blog sharing personal opinions and practical explanations about home loans in Singapore. The aim is to make mortgage topics easier for the average Singaporean to understand.

The blog is designed for first-time HDB buyers, BTO applicants, existing HDB owners considering refinancing and borrowers comparing HDB and bank loans.

The views shared here are personal opinions for general education. They are not personalised financial advice. Readers should confirm current rates, eligibility requirements and package terms with the relevant institution before making a decision.

Start with a decision

  1. Understand the loan structure.
  2. Compare the full package, not only the headline rate.
  3. Use the calculator to test your assumptions.
  4. Check the latest official terms before acting.
